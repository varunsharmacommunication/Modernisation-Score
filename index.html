    How this score is calculated

    The Modernization Score starts from a neutral baseline of 55 and moves up or down based on your platform's currency and customization depth, integration footprint, the actual driver behind the conversation, business criticality, and timeline. A genuine functional gap — something your current platform truly cannot do — is the one input that can pull the score decisively toward Migrate, because it's the one reason on this list that modernizing usually can't fix. Oracle has repeatedly extended Premier Support for PeopleSoft, E-Business Suite, and JD Edwards (currently through at least 2037), so end-of-life concern alone is not treated as a strong reason to migrate. This tool gives a directional read to support your own diligence, not a substitute for a formal assessment.
