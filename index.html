How we calculated this

Modernization Score: starts from a neutral baseline and adjusts for how customized your current system is, what's actually driving the conversation (board pressure, perceived end-of-life, a genuine functional gap, or just exploring), your institution type and size, and whether a migration decision is already in motion. A genuine functional gap is the one input that can pull the score toward "Migrate" — because it's the one reason on this list that modernizing usually can't fix.

Migration cost: a base per-user replacement estimate, plus 22–48% for hidden costs most RFPs don't itemize — dual-running two systems, data migration surprises, retraining, and the productivity dip during cutover. The exact range scales with how customized your current environment is.

Modernization cost: a base estimate for layering AI and automation onto your existing platform, plus 10–15% for integration and change management.

This is a planning estimate, not a quote. For migrations already well underway, or platforms with genuinely unique constraints, treat this as a starting point for a conversation — including, sometimes, the conversation about why migrating is still the right call.

Common questions

Is PeopleSoft, E-Business Suite, or JD Edwards support actually ending?

No. Oracle has repeatedly extended Premier Support for PeopleSoft, most recently through at least 2036, and JD Edwards Premier Support runs through at least 2037. The platforms most institutions run today aren't being discontinued.

What is a Migration Tax?

The hidden cost most institutions never calculate when they replace a working ERP instead of modernizing it — on top of the quoted project cost.

Does modernizing always beat migrating?

No — if you've genuinely outgrown what your current platform can do, migrating can be the right call. This tool is built to say so when that's true.
